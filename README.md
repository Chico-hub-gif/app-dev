# My Favorite Movie: *Arrival* (2016)

## Overview

**Arrival** is a science-fiction drama directed by **Denis Villeneuve**. It's based on
Ted Chiang's short story *"Story of Your Life."* Instead of a typical alien-invasion
action film, it's a quiet, thoughtful story about **language, time, and communication**.

## Plot (No Major Spoilers)

1. Twelve mysterious alien spacecraft land in different places around the world.
2. Linguist Louise Banks is recruited by the U.S. military to communicate with them.
3. She and physicist Ian Donnelly try to decode the aliens' circular written language.
4. As tensions rise between nations, Louise races to understand what the aliens want.

---

## Why I Love It

- The **story** makes you think long after it ends
- The **visuals** are calm, eerie, and beautiful
- The **music** by Jóhann Jóhannsson creates a haunting mood
- It shows that *understanding each other* matters more than fighting
  - Nested point: it makes language feel like a superpower

> "Arrival proves that a sci-fi movie doesn't need explosions to be exciting —
> just a great idea and a lot of heart."
> — My personal review

---

## Fun Facts

- The aliens are called **Heptapods** because they have *seven limbs*.
- Their writing is made of ~~straight lines~~ **circular ink symbols** called *logograms*.
- The film won the **Academy Award for Best Sound Editing**.
